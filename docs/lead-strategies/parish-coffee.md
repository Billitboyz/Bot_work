# Sales Strategy — Parish Coffee

## Goal
Use a simple brochure-style site to convert local discovery into calls, walk-ins, and catering leads.

## Offer Positioning
- "Elegant local coffee spot with quality beans and easy contact."
- Keep friction low with clear contact/map/hours.

## Funnel
1. **Search/Maps/Social** → clean brochure page
2. **Primary CTA**: View menu
3. **Secondary CTA**: Contact / Book catering
4. **Lead capture**: catering enquiry strip on-page

## Conversion Assets
- Story + menu snapshot + location block
- Contact strip with one-tap actions (call/email/map)
- Catering quick-enquiry form

## Sales Actions
- Add catering package PDF + minimum order guidance
- Add event/office catering testimonials
- Follow-up SLA: reply to enquiries within 1 hour business time

## KPI Targets (30 days)
- +30% contact clicks
- +10 catering enquiries
- +20% map route starts
